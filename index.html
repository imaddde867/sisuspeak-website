Lack of Real-time Feedback

Most language apps don't provide immediate, constructive feedback on your spoken Finnish, hindering natural progression.

Slow Progress & Frustration

Despite significant study time, many learners struggle to achieve conversational fluency in Finnish, leading to demotivation.

Impersonal Learning Paths

Anxiety Around Speaking
